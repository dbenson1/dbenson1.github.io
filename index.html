About

I help organizations prove they are secure, not just say it. My work sits where governance meets technical risk: I map controls to NIST 800-53 and ISO 27001, build System Security Plans and POA&Ms, and turn raw scan data from Tenable and Qualys into remediation plans that engineers can act on and leadership can understand. Before cybersecurity, I spent years in operations and project coordination. That's where I learned to manage deadlines, document processes clearly and keep many stakeholders aligned. I now apply those skills to audit readiness, risk assessments and vulnerability triage. I'm comfortable with frameworks and with findings, and I translate between the two. I'm looking for a GRC Analyst, IT Risk Analyst or Vulnerability Management Analyst role where I can reduce real risk and keep audit evidence clean.
