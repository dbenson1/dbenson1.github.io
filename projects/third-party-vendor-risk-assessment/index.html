What I did

Assessed a fictional cloud payroll vendor. I sent a SIG Lite-style security questionnaire, reviewed the vendor's SOC 2 Type II report for exceptions and complementary user entity controls (CUECs), scored residual risk, and recommended contract clauses for breach notification and right to audit.

What I learned

A SOC 2 report shifts some responsibility back to you. The CUECs are where most organizations miss their own obligations.
